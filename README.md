# E-commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI

I am developing an interactive E-commerce Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and transform raw data into meaningful business insights. My main objective is to understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and support data-driven decision-making through effective visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

I am using four major KPIs in my dashboard: **Total Sales, Total Profit, Quantity Sold, and Average Order Value (AOV)**. These metrics are helping me understand overall sales performance, financial contribution, product movement, and average order value. By analyzing these KPIs together, I am exploring how sales and profitability are connected and how business performance can be evaluated from different perspectives.

I am analyzing monthly profit trends to understand changes in profitability over time. I am also examining profit by product sub-category and quantity sold by category to identify differences in product performance and demand. Additionally, I am exploring profit by payment mode, average order value across states, and sales contributions from individual customers. These visualizations are helping me investigate patterns and identify areas that may require further analysis.

I am incorporating interactive **Quarter and State filters** to make my dashboard more dynamic and user-friendly. These filters allow users to explore selected reporting periods and geographic markets, compare business performance, and examine specific segments of the data.

Through this project, I am learning how to convert business data into an understandable visual story. I am focusing on questions such as whether sales are generating profit, which product categories contribute to performance, how purchasing patterns vary across regions, and how individual customers contribute to total sales. I am using these questions to guide my analysis rather than making assumptions without sufficient evidence.

I am strengthening my skills in **Power BI, KPI development, data visualization, business intelligence, analytical thinking, and data storytelling**. I am also improving my ability to present complex information in a clear and structured format.

Overall, I am building this dashboard to demonstrate how data analytics can help businesses monitor performance, explore opportunities, and make informed decisions. This project is an important step in developing my practical data analytics skills and building a professional portfolio for future opportunities in the field.
